# Design direction

Audience: shoppers looking for skincare and personal-care products. The primary tasks are
finding a product and adding it to a cart.

Translate the supplied 1920px desktop Home Page and ALL Products frames into Blade layouts.
Keep their layout, section order, Montserrat typography, and supplied demonstration imagery.
For the branded interface, the user's approved Secret Herbals identity supersedes Figma's
blue/lavender colors. Use a botanical-heritage palette: forest `#234936` for primary actions,
deep forest `#183A2B` for depth, antique gold `#C79A4A` for selective accents, soft gold
`#F2E8D4`, sage `#E8EEE6` / `#F2F5EF`, charcoal `#171614`, warm ivory `#F7F4ED`, and
charcoal text `#25231F`. Use the supplied logo as a transparent cutout in the deep-forest header
and footer, with warm-ivory/gold header controls and a warm-ivory navigation bar. Keep content
surfaces light enough for shopping readability.
Use responsive grids and collapsed navigation on smaller screens; Figma has no mobile frames.

The brand lockup is Secret Herbals by Shazia Saeed. Figma product photos and listing values
remain development demos as authorized; a visible demo-catalog label separates them from
approved Herbal product data. Avoid presenting Figma customer counts, before/after stories,
or reviews as verified Herbal claims. No third-party Jenpharm navigation links are carried
into the app.

The homepage follows the supplied frame order: full-width campaign hero, trust strip,
category tiles, best-seller carousel and filters, innovation carousel, before/after sample
stories, skin-analysis banner, review-photo carousel, offer artwork, video-poster favorites,
brand quote, press-logo marquee, and a multi-column footer. Carousel arrows, touch scrolling,
filter tabs, search, mobile menu, product links, and cart forms remain usable at desktop,
tablet, and mobile widths. Figma review photos and poster-only video cards are identified
as samples; video play opens a static-preview explanation because no video files were supplied.
